About

The long road still has songs in it.

Robert Parlee’s story runs from Pasadena rock bands and major-label chapters to the direct craft of solo songwriting in Adin, California.

Short Bio Draft

Robert Parlee is a singer, songwriter, and performer whose career spans major-label rock history and current Americana-rooted solo work. His credits include Billboard charting with Isle of Man, MTV airplay for “Desperate Surrender,” and recordings with Pasha/CBS and Curb Records.
