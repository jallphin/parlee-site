About Robert Owen Parlee

The story starts in Pasadena and keeps moving north.

Singer, songwriter, pianist, and guitarist Robert Owen Parlee has moved through Southern California rock bands, major-label releases, film credits, tribute-band stages, and a current home-studio chapter in Northern California.

Biography

Robert Owen Parlee is a singer, songwriter, and musician on piano, electric guitar, and acoustic guitar. His career started as a teenager in the Pasadena, California area in the band Pictures, leading to gigs and record contracts. Parlee signed with Pasha/CBS Records and became part of Isle of Man with Ron Aniello, James Carter, and Ronnie Lee Sage, produced by Spencer Proffer.

Isle of Man released one album with Parlee as lead vocal under the studio-issued pseudonym “Robere Parlez.” The album peaked at #110 on Billboard’s Hot 200. The video for “Desperate Surrender” was played on MTV and MTV2, and “Am I Forgiven” charted at #90 in 1986.

Parlee’s next contract was with Curb Records in Lonesome Romeos with Ron Aniello and Phillip Kennard, produced by Toby Scott. The Lonesome Romeos released two albums: The Lonesome Romeos, with Parlee on backing vocals and keys, and Seven Years in Bed, with Parlee on lead vocals.

His label work led to film credits in Teen Wolf Too, Major League, and Wedding Bell Blues. He later collaborated with SoCal songwriter John Jensen, appeared at Staples Center and in a TV pilot with Clementine, played with Colour My World, and performed solo and with The Terry McRaven Band in Big Bear. He now plays local venues and creates original compositions with his wife, Julie, from a home studio in the mountains of Northern California.
